Default template: Classic Blue v7

Apply these editorial defaults to both new resumes and AI refinements. The fixed renderer supplies the v7 typography, margins, pale-blue section rules and single-column skill paragraphs. Do not recreate layout in text or change fonts to force a fit.

- Target up to two US Letter pages. For substantial senior experience, use two balanced pages. Preserve relevant history rather than aggressively summarizing it to one page; never invent filler or repeat content to fill space. Do not force a fixed page break based on an employer name. Preserve deliberate pageBreakBefore flags during refinement unless the user requests pagination changes.
- Order: header, concise Professional Summary, Technical Skills, all supplied Certifications, Experience newest first, optional Projects, Education last. LinkedIn, GitHub and credential links remain clickable and source-exact.
- For infrastructure, Azure, cloud, systems administration or DevOps applications, use the six categories below when the source supports each. Keep technologies relevant and lists concise. Omit an unsupported/empty category rather than filling it with invented skills; use other categories only when the target is outside this direction.
  1. Cloud Platforms & Infrastructure
  2. DevOps & Infrastructure as Code
  3. Containers & Orchestration
  4. Systems & Networking
  5. Scripting & Automation
  6. Security, Monitoring & Recovery
- Prioritize source-supported Azure, Terraform, Azure DevOps, Kubernetes, Linux, Windows Server, PowerShell, Python and networking. Include PowerShell only when supplied by the candidate; do not invent a PowerShell responsibility, project or job association. A keyword in a JD or an adjacent technology is not evidence of experience or a skill.
- Use Kubernetes in employer-facing prose instead of K3s. Do not change source URLs. Keep internal qualifiers such as "official title to confirm" or "functional role" out of the final PDF. Preserve the underlying job title and actual self-employed status; do not upgrade rank or ownership.
- For a current independent infrastructure/automation role, aim for 4-5 concise bullets covering supported Linux administration, containers, scripting, API/systems integration and troubleshooting. Emphasize transferable engineering; minimize retailer, catalog, pricing and business-application detail. Do not imply enterprise cloud deployments, external clients or a large engineering team without source evidence.
- Strengthen cloud/DevOps experience with precise verbs for personally performed work, such as administered, assessed, mapped, developed, configured, diagnosed and verified. Retain assisted/shared responsibility when appropriate. Avoid repeatedly starting bullets with Supported or Contributed; do not replace these with led, owned or architected unless supported by the source.
- Preserve the stated cloud migration direction exactly. Do not reverse source and destination to improve apparent fit. Keep facts requiring verification out of employer-facing prose rather than presenting them as confirmed; any requested review checklist belongs outside the resume.
- Preserve every supplied certification name, verification URL and explicitly supplied historical date. A renewal year is not proof of current validity. Do not call credentials active/current unless the source verifies it. Do not invent completion or expiry dates. CKA/CKS status requires issuer verification when unconfirmed; never infer it from a badge name or the current year.
- Preserve factual employers, dates, education and career progression. Keep the summary credible and reflect only source-stated tenure; never manufacture uninterrupted employment or unsupported metrics.
